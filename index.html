Annual vehicle safety inspection

Most vehicles must pass a safety inspection once every 12 months. Our certified centers in Berlin, San Francisco and Sydney do it for you.

What is checked

Brakes and parking brake, lights and horn, steering and suspension, tires and wheels, mirrors, windshield and wipers, exhaust and emissions equipment, seats, seat belts and airbags, doors, hood latch, floor pan and license plate light.

How it works

Visit a certified inspection station, the old sticker is removed, a certified inspector checks the safety items – if your vehicle passes, a new sticker goes on the windshield.

If it does not pass

You may get a rejection sticker or notice. Repair the listed defects and return for re-inspection within 15 days – Quinn books the re-inspection for you.

Frequently asked questions

Quinn can answer these and more on the phone.

Can I change my service center location?

Yes. You can usually change locations before final confirmation, though you may need to reselect available services or times.

Will I get a confirmation email or SMS?

Yes. A confirmation is typically sent by email, and optional text reminders may be available.

What time should I arrive for my service?

Arrive about 10–15 minutes early so check-in is quick and easy.

Are loaner vehicles available during service?

Loaner availability varies by location and service type. Check with your service center when booking.

Can I book if I am not sure what my vehicle needs?

Yes. You can choose a general inspection or diagnostic visit and discuss symptoms with the technician.
